About Sophia

Where Wellness
Meets Ambition

I'm Sophia Laurent — a certified Wellness Coach, Serial Entrepreneur, Business Mentor, and International Public Speaker. For over a decade, I've dedicated my life to helping high-achievers, executives, and visionary women build businesses and lives they truly love.

My approach blends ancient wisdom with modern strategy — creating a unique methodology that addresses the whole person: mind, body, business, and spirit. I believe that sustainable success comes only when your inner world is aligned with your outer ambitions.

Having built multiple 7-figure businesses from the ground up, I bring real-world entrepreneurial expertise alongside deep wellness philosophy. My clients don't just grow their businesses — they transform their relationship with success itself.

The Journey

A Path Built on
Experience & Excellence

2012

The Beginning

Started as a certified yoga and mindfulness instructor, working with burnout professionals in London. Discovered the intersection of wellness and business performance.

2015

First Venture

Founded her first wellness brand, scaling it to £2M in revenue within 18 months. Recognized by Forbes as a rising voice in holistic entrepreneurship.

2018

Going Global

Launched the acclaimed "Lead From Within" program, reaching executives across 20+ countries. Delivered keynotes at TEDx, Davos Fringe, and the Global Women's Summit.

2021

The Academy

Founded the Sophia Laurent Academy — an online platform for elite coaching and mentorship programs. Enrolled over 500 high-achievers across 30 countries.

2024

The Next Chapter

Published her bestselling book "The Quiet Power". Continues to mentor, speak, and inspire from her studios in London, Dubai, and Singapore.
